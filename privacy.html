MailMaid Privacy Policy

Information We Access

MailMaid accesses Gmail information necessary to apply the cleanup rules configured by the user.

How Information Is Used

Gmail information is used only to perform the Gmail cleanup actions requested by the user.

Data Sharing

MailMaid does not sell or share users' Gmail information with third parties.

Data Storage

MailMaid stores its configuration and settings locally using Chrome storage.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the publisher through the contact email provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
